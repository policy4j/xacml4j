#### 1. The Seven Cases: XACML 3.0 Validity and Coverage of Sol's `pr15` Workload Gaps

1. **`integer-ranges` ([`RulesCorpus.java:163-165`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`236-245`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`)):** Covers `pr15` opportunity 7 (range and overlap targets). In XACML 3.0 `<Match>`, the literal `<AttributeValue>` is the first argument and the `<AttributeDesignator>` element is the second, so `integer-less-than-or-equal(10i, size)` and `integer-greater-than(10i + 15, size)` encode the half-open interval $10i \le \text{size} < 10i + 15$. For $\text{size} = 10(n-1) + 2$, rule $0$ matches when $n = 1$, and both rules $n-2$ and $n-1$ match in the overlap $[10(n-1), 10(n-2)+15)$ when $n \ge 2$; under `first-applicable`, rule $\max(0, n-2)$ wins.
2. **`date-ranges` ([`RulesCorpus.java:166-168`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`247-256`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`)):** Covers `xs:date` interval targets (`date-less-than-or-equal` / `date-greater-than` over days from `2024-01-01`) under `deny-overrides`. When $n = 1$, rule $0$ permits; when $n \ge 2$, rules $n-2$ and $n-1$ both match and have opposite parity (one `Permit`, one `Deny`), so `deny-overrides` always decides `Deny`.
3. **`repeated-conditions` ([`RulesCorpus.java:169-172`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`258-270`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`)):** Covers `pr15` opportunity 6 (prepared condition plans and subexpression reuse). Only rule $n-1$ matches `department-(n-1)` and permits.
4. **`multi-valued` ([`RulesCorpus.java:149-153`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`173-175`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`272-286`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`)):** Covers multi-valued request bags across both target indexing (`role` bag with $\min(n, 8)$ matching roles plus `role-unknown`) and higher-order bag functions (`all-of(Function(string-starts-with), "group-", group)` and `any-of(Function(string-equal), "group-3", group)` over an 8-element `group` bag). 5. **`narrow` and `wide` ([`RulesCorpus.java:101-105`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`154-161`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`176-179`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`), [`288-292`](`xacml-jmh/src/main/java/org/xacml4j/jmh/corpus/RulesCorpus.java`)):** Covers `pr15` opportunity 2 / D37 request-key projection (wide requests with unread attributes and `IncludeInResult="true"` attributes across all 4 categories).
