Refuted or deprioritized ideas:
- "The 14.5% `ImmutableList$1.get` means the Target child lists are slow": the stack is the bag's multiset iterator (`ImmutableMultiset$1.next`, `AbstractIndexedListIterator.next`, `ImmutableList$1.get`, 1,469 samples, collapsed S), not the Match, AnyOf, AllOf lists; those cost 3.4% (H7).
- Rewriting `DenyOverrides.doCombine`: self 1.1% on S, 0.6% on F; nothing to gain, as PF3 found.
- `ImmutableMultiset.asList()` as a quick fix for `Match`: for a one-value bag it yields a `SingletonImmutableList` whose iterator is `Iterators$9` again, and the construction cost on R stays; superseded by H1.
- Precomputing request flags per `RequestContext`: real callers build the request per decision, so the work moves to the builder (D9's reasoning holds); only the cheaper structures of H7 and H9 and the one content-selector boolean are real gains.
- `static final boolean DEBUG = LOG.isDebugEnabled()`: zero cost but ignores runtime level changes; H4 prefers removal plus a per-decision snapshot.
- Replacing the designator memo's `ConcurrentHashMap` by a `HashMap`: refused by D5's PIP-callback contract; presizing and views (H9) keep the contract.
- Caching `Value$OtherValue.hashCode`: `value` is a protected non-final field (`C/types/Value.java:108`) and the D9 review refused such a cache for `SemanticIdentifier`; the varargs array can still go (`31*(31+value.hashCode())+type.hashCode()`, −4.7% of R bytes, −1 to −2% R time) and H1 stops calling it on construction.
- `-XX:-StackTraceInThrowable` for H8: a JVM-wide flag that hides every stack trace; the engine-local override with a property is the right scope.
- A hash pre-check in `AttributeDesignatorKey.equals`: the concurrent map already compares hashes first; the cost is the byte compare of equal strings, so interning (H6) is the fix.
- Treating every `itable stub` sample as function-call megamorphism: on all three sets the callers are the context forwarders and `isEnclosed`, not the function dispatch (collapsed S, R, F, callers of `itable stub`).
