   Spec Audit & Test Design: Tasks 4.1 & 4.2

  Core Specification https://docs.oasis-open.org/xacml/3.0/xacml-3.0-core-spec-os-en.html (§5.25, §5.29,
  §5.30, §5.58, §5.59, §7.3.5, §7.11, §7.19.3) and the current xacml-core and xacml-pdp implementations.
